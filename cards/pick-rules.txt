# Pick rules updated 2026-10-26
# The cumulative chart is the record. Read it before the card.
# Through 2026-10-25 the running total is -$112.81. It never crossed $0.
# The low was -$220.07 on 10/08. The best the line got was -$18.39 on 10/02.
# The climb back to -$59.26 on 10/20 was real, then 10/21 gave back $65.22 in one day.
# Last 7 settled days are -$43.33. Last 3 sum +$1.29 only because 10/23 was +$42.94.
# 10/24 and 10/25 gave back $41.65. Recompute these figures from cards/calendar.json every morning.
# The six-play cap is gone. It was fit to one night: October 23 went 5-1 on six plays.
# A sharp does not cut a good number to hit a count, and does not invent a bet to fill one.
# Old rules that picked a side first are gone with it: every dog of 5.5 or more,
# one dog per sport, no college spread inside 7, no moneyline shorter than +140,
# one under per day, and every must-play team. Those rules picked the side, then went looking for a reason.
# Side-plus-total parlays stay off. They were 8-17, -$15, from October 12 through October 23.
# A day with games and no bets does not improve anything. It only avoids a result.
# Passing because two books agree is not a price. That is what emptied October 25 on the first pass.

Read cards/prices.txt before making a number. That file is the training log. A bad night does not change the card. The log does.

1. Make the number first. For every game on the slate, write a side and a total before looking at which side the old card would have wanted. The book number is the comparison, not the pick.
2. Bet only the gap. NFL or NBA side: the book is at least 1.5 points off the made number. College side: 2.5 points. NHL puck line or MLB run line: the book is at least a half point off, or the moneyline is at least 15 cents off the made price. NFL or NBA total: 2 points. College total: 3 points. MLB total: 1 run. NHL total: 0.5 goals. If the gap is smaller than that, pass that game. A favorite is allowed. A dog is allowed. A short price is allowed when the made price is shorter than the book. Do not pass a sport because the books agree with each other.
3. One bet per game. Bet the market with the bigger gap. No parlay, no second leg, no cross-game ticket. A parlay adds the book's juice on top of the juice already in each leg.
4. No team is a must bet. Indiana, Army, Navy, Air Force, the Pacers, the Colts, Purdue, and Notre Dame are games. They are bets only when rule 2 passes. Read the injury report before the number. If the best player is out, make the number with him out. Do not move the number after seeing the book's side.
5. Do not replay a ticket because it won last night. Do not fade a ticket because it lost last night.
6. There is no cap and no quota. Two plays is a full card if only two numbers are off. Fourteen is a full card if fourteen numbers are off. Write No Picks only when the made number also says every game is inside the gap. Two books posting the same number is not that test.
7. Every play on the card shows three figures: the made number, the book number, and the gap. If those three are not written, it is not a bet.
8. Stake stays $10 until that market has 30 graded rows in cards/prices.txt and the average close beat the bet. Then the biggest gap on the card can be $15. Nothing is bigger than $15 until a market is 50 graded bets and still beating the close.
9. TRAINING: After the closes are saved, append one row per bet to cards/prices.txt:
   date | sport | market | made | bet | close | clv
   Market is side or total. CLV is the close minus the bet, in points for a spread or a total and in cents for a moneyline, signed so a positive number means the close moved toward the bet.
   The next card reads the log first. Adjust a market's made number only when the last 30 graded rows in that sport and market average negative. Move the number toward the close by the size of that average, and write the adjustment at the top of the card. Do not adjust off a sample smaller than 30. Do not drop a market for a losing record if the CLV is positive. Do not keep a market for a winning record if the CLV is negative over 30.
10. SLATE FILE: cards/scoreboard.json is the full day's scoreboard. Leave it in place when the card is combined. cards/pick-slate.json games are only the games on that day's card, and every other scoreboard game goes in passed. Never write an empty pick-slate.json. An empty file takes the picks off the Seasons card.
11. If today's part file already has bet lines, and each line shows the made number, the book number, and the gap, keep those lines. Do not replace a posted bet with No Picks.
12. CHART. Before any number, add up cards/calendar.json from the first settled day through the last. That sum is the cumulative line.
    If the sum is under $0, stake stays $10. Rule 8 cannot raise it.
    If the last three settled days sum to a loss, add 1 point to every side gap in rule 2, add 1 point to every football, basketball, and baseball total gap, and add 0.5 goals to the NHL total gap. Do not add a bet to get the money back.
    If the last three settled days sum to a win, use the gaps in rule 2. Do not make them smaller because the line rose.
    One green day does not cross the line out of the red. Do not loosen anything until the running total itself is above $0.

INTERIM NUMBER. Use this until that sport and market has 30 graded rows. Then stop, and use rule 9.
This is a record formula, not a trained price. It exists so a day with games produces bets the log can grade.
Do not mark any play High until those 30 rows exist. Edge still goes on every play that clears.
The formula is a side number only. Do not add a total until a total is made the same way.

NFL. Home spread = (home win% - away win%) * 14 + 2.5. A neutral site uses 0 instead of 2.5. Win% is wins plus half a tie, divided by games played. Bet when the gap against the posted spread is at least 3 points. If the page does not show the juice, the price is -110.
NBA. Home spread = (home win% - away win%) * 10 + 3. Bet when the gap is at least 5 points. Early-season records are short. Still bet the games that clear. Pass the games that do not. Price -110 if the juice is not posted.
NHL. Points percentage is points divided by games played times 2. Fair share is one team's points percentage divided by the sum of both. Move 4 percentage points to the home team. Convert that probability to an American price. Bet when BetMGM is at least 15 cents better than that price.
CFB and CBB. Home spread = (home win% - away win%) * 17 + 3. Bet when the gap is at least 4 points. Price -110 if the juice is not posted.
MLB. Use season win% the same way as the NHL moneyline, with a 4 percentage point home bump. Bet the moneyline when it is 15 cents better than that price, or the run line when it is at least half a run off. If there is no game, do not write a file.
