These rules override the 2026-10-12 habit of carding every dog of 5.5 or more and pairing it with the under.

1. One dog spread per sport per day. Not three.
2. A dog spread of +8 or more is not a play in the first 10 games of a season. Pass the game.
3. Do not build a same-game parlay of a dog spread plus the under. A blowout cashes the under and kills the dog. The Bulls game did that: under won, dog lost by 43, parlay lost.
4. One named under is allowed. Do not also bet that game's dog.
5. A moneyline dog must be +140 or longer. +102 is a pick'em. Pass it. The Flyers were +166 and won. The Panthers were +102 and lost by 4.
6. A college spread inside 7 is not a play. Sam Houston +2.5 lost by 6.
7. A college total under 48 is not a play. 45.5 went to 54.
8. A standalone NHL total is not a play. A total is allowed only as the second leg with a moneyline already priced +140 or longer.
9. If no number fits, write No Picks for that sport. Do not force a dog onto a bad number.
10. NBA MUST PLAY: If the Indiana Pacers play today, that game is on the card. Do not write No Picks while they play. One Pacers straight. While Tyrese Haliburton is active, card the Pacers spread. Do not lay a Pacers moneyline of -150 or shorter. Do not pair a Pacers dog with the under. If the Pacers do not play, do not invent a game.

If he is questionable or on a minutes limit, take the dog or the under unless the Pacers spread is small. Resume the Pacers side only when he is playing with no restriction.
