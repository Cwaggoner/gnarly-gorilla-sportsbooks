      Slips

      Today, yesterday, and every open slip. Pull Odds & Close uses the Odds API key and stores the Pinnacle close with the other books. Odds is the price at the start of the game. Close is the next price after it starts. Sharp CLV is that price against Pinnacle with the juice removed. NSC in that column means No Sharp Close. The time under the date is when the slip was written. A game that has not started stays open.

      Live prices need the Odds API key on Add A Bet. Close needs a paid subscription. CLV is no-vig: the book's cut is removed, and a moved spread or total is counted. A matched -110 close is about -4.5%, not zero. When the other side was not saved, it is estimated at a -110 hold until Plot stores the real pair. A free key loads the board only. A saved list returns on refresh with no new call.
