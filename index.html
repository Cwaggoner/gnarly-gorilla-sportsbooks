Injury Reports

  • Sport keeps one category.
  • Posted hides teams with nothing filed, so the list stays short.
  • Out is a player who will not play.
  • May still play is questionable or day to day.
  • Red means out.
  • Amber means he may still play.
  • The page pulls the public report when the card loads and again every few minutes.
  • A filing pushed today is what everyone sees. Before that push, the page reads the public report.
  • The times below are when a new filing is actually due, in Eastern time.

About This Ledger

Gnarly Gorilla Sportsbooks is a free browser bet ledger for the daily Gnarly Gorilla pick cards. It tracks American-odds slips for NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college basketball, and college football at BetMGM, Caesars, DraftKings, Fanatics, FanDuel, and HardRock. Bets stay in this browser. The page does not log into a sportsbook and does not place wagers.

Bet Responsibly. Gambling involves risk. Wagers can lose the full stake. This ledger is a record of picks and results, not a sportsbook, not betting advice, and not an offer to place a wager. You must be 21 or older where sports betting is legal. If gambling is causing problems, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
