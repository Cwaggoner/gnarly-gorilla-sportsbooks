About this ledger


Gnarly Gorilla Sportsbooks is a free browser bet ledger for the daily Gnarly Gorilla pick cards. It tracks American-odds slips for NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college basketball, and college football at BetMGM, DraftKings, Fanatics, FantasySports, and HardRock. Bets stay in this browser. The page does not log into a sportsbook and does not place wagers.
